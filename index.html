01 · The tools

Two small tools. Free to use, today.

One keeps a solo run on budget. One keeps a team's agents on the plan everyone agreed to. Install either in one command, and remove it just as fast.

FreeSolo devClaude Code

lake-meter

A model for every step, a cap that stops and asks.

Before you approve a plan, see the right model and a $ range for each step. During the run, your cap pauses Claude and asks before it spends more. Everything stays on your machine.

$npx lake-meter init
Explore lake-meter Source
Approved by a teammateplan v5c19e7a2
FreeTeamsClaude Code & Cursor

lake-gate

Your coding agent waits for a real yes.

The agent's plan lands in the repo, and the build waits until a teammate approves that exact version. Edit the plan and the approval expires. Runs in your repo and on GitHub.

$npx lake-gate init
Explore lake-gate Live demo

02 · Open core

Open source at the core. Team features on top.

You can read every line the tools run inside your repo. That's the point.

  • Free and MIT licensed

    lake-meter and lake-gate are open source and free to use, on your own or with your whole team.

  • Runs where you already work

    lake-meter runs fully on your machine. lake-gate runs in your repo and on GitHub. No Lake account, no Lake server, no telemetry in the tools.

  • A team tier, on top Paid

    We're building a paid tier for teams: shared budgets, approval history and one view across repos. The free tools never depend on it.
