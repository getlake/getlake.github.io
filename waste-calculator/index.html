Claude Code waste calculator

Paste your ccusage JSON and see where the money went: spend by model, your most expensive sessions, an estimate of what was avoidable, and how much of it ran with no approved plan.

Your data never leaves this page. Parsing and every calculation happen in your browser. There is no upload and nothing is stored. The page's Content-Security-Policy only allows one outgoing connection, to our privacy-friendly page-view counter (GoatCounter), which receives the page address and nothing you paste. You can verify it in your browser's network tab.

1. Get your export

npx ccusage@latest claude session --json --breakdown
npx ccusage@latest claude daily --json

Either one works; both together give the fullest picture. Add --since YYYYMMDD to limit the period. Project names appear in the output, so rename them first if they are sensitive (they are only shown on your screen).

How it works, and its limits

  • It reads the schema ccusage prints (sessions or daily, with modelBreakdowns) and the multi-agent session shape. Costs are the ones ccusage computed from your local logs.
  • The estimates are rules of thumb, shown in full next to each number. They say where to look, not what you could have saved. Your own judgement beats them.
  • The "no approved plan" figure is 100% by construction: an export cannot show an approval, so without a plan gate nothing was approved.
  • If you use a subscription plan, ccusage costs are API-equivalent prices, not what you were billed.
