Privacy Policy

We collect as little as we can. The Lake software has no telemetry, and your code stays in your repository.

Effective 27 November 2026

Who is responsible

Lake is responsible for the data described here. Contact: hello@getlake.dev.

The Lake software

  • No telemetry or analytics. The command-line tool does not report usage to us.
  • Plans, comments and approvals are stored in your repository and on GitHub. They are not sent to us.
  • The only network calls go to the GitHub API, using your own GitHub login or token, to read and write your pull requests.
  • License checks run offline. The trial start date and any license key are stored on your machine.

This website

  • Visit counts: we use GoatCounter to count page views. It sets no cookies and does not store your IP address or build a profile of you.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Hosted review app: if you use it, your GitHub token is kept in your browser and sent only to api.github.com, never to us.

When you buy

Orders are handled by our online reseller Polar Software Inc. (polar.sh), the Merchant of Record for all our orders. It collects your name, email, billing address and payment details to process the order and taxes, under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email, country, plan and order status, so we can provide your license and support. We never see your full card details.

When you contact us

If you email us, we use your message and email address to reply. If you post in our GitHub Discussions or issues, that content is public on GitHub.

Waste report requests

If you send us a usage export for a waste report, we read only its token, model and cost metadata, use it only to write your report, and delete the files after we send the report. We keep your name and email to reply, like other support email. Please do not send raw logs that contain prompts or code.

Waste calculator

The free waste calculator at /waste-calculator/ runs entirely in your browser. The JSON you paste or drop is parsed on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is not stored. Like every page, page views are counted with privacy-friendly analytics, which does not include what you paste.

Why we use data

To provide licenses and support (performing our agreement with you), to keep records required by tax and accounting law, and to understand site traffic in aggregate (our legitimate interest). We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Order records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically up to 7 years. Support email: up to 2 years after the conversation ends. Visit counts are aggregate.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@getlake.dev. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your data protection authority. In Israel, that is the Privacy Protection Authority.

Service providers

GitHub (hosting and repositories), GoatCounter (visit counts) and Polar Software Inc. (orders and payments, as Merchant of Record). Some of them process data outside your country, under their own safeguards.

Changes

We will post any changes here with a new effective date.
