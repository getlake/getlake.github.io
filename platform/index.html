01 · Early prototype

What we're building.

Ticket vs. reality

Will flag tickets whose status doesn't match the PRs, CI runs and deploys behind them, with the evidence linked.

Blocker loop

Every blocker will get an owner and an agreed date, with follow-ups until it's cleared.

Trade-off planner

A new request will turn into scenarios (what moves, how much delay, engineering and AI cost) before you approve it.

Linear+GitHubLake

Lake is designed to sit on top of Linear, not replace it, so there's nothing to migrate. No integrations are live yet.
